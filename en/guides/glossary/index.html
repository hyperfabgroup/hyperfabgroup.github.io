Persian–English Manufacturing Glossary

Updated: 17 November 2026

This glossary pairs the terms you hear in Iranian workshops and on supplier calls with the English terms used on drawings, datasheets, standards and quotes. If a Tehran shop talks about a dahan-mahi cut or rang-e kure-i and your drawing says coping or powder coating, find the section, read across the table and note the common Persian spelling variants.

General drawing and ordering terms in Persian

A handful of words come up in every order regardless of process: drawing, tolerance, batch size and file format. Getting these right in both languages keeps a quote request unambiguous and makes sure the shop builds what the drawing actually says.

EnglishPersianShort explanation
Technical drawing; shop drawingنقشه (نقشه‌ی فنی، نقشه‌ی کارگاهی)The 2D drawing with views, dimensions, tolerances, material and finish that the part is made and checked against.
3D model; CAD modelمدل سه‌بعدیThe solid model the cutting, turning or printing program is generated from.
Toleranceتلرانس (تولرانس)The permitted deviation of a dimension from its nominal value.
General tolerance (e.g. ISO 2768)تلرانس عمومیThe tolerance stated in the title block for every dimension that has no tolerance of its own.
Batch; production runتیراژ (سفارش تیراژی، تولید سری)The number of identical parts in one order, over which tooling and set-up cost is spread.
Sample; prototypeنمونه (نمونه‌ی اولیه، پروتوتایپ)A trial part made before series production to check dimensions and fit and to get sign-off.
Request for quotation (RFQ); quoteاستعلام قیمت (پیش‌فاکتور)A price request with drawing, material, quantity and finish; the reply is a pro forma or quote.
Material; material gradeجنس (متریال)The exact alloy or polymer grade, not just "steel" or "plastic".
STEP / IGES fileفایل STEP و IGESNeutral 3D exchange formats that open in any CAD package.
DXF fileفایل DXFA vector 2D format, the usual input for sheet laser cutting and punching.
STL fileفایل STLA triangulated surface model, the usual input for 3D printing.
Bill of materials (BOM)فهرست قطعاتA table of the parts in an assembly with the quantity and material of each.

Sheet and profile cutting and forming terms

Sheet and profile work has the widest spread of names: one shop calls a cut dahan-mahi (fish-mouth), the next calls it zin-asbi (saddle). HyperFab offers sheet laser cutting and bending online, and tube and profile laser cutting, punching and punching die making as industrial orders.

EnglishPersianShort explanation
Laser cutting; sheet laser cuttingبرش لیزر (برش لیزر ورق)Cutting flat sheet with a laser beam straight from a 2D file, with no tooling.
Tube and profile laser cutting; tube laserبرش لیزر پروفیل و لولهCutting to length plus holes, slots and end shapes on box section and tube in one program and one set-up.
Punchingپانچ (پانچینگ)Making holes, slots and forms in sheet or tube with a press stroke and a die; suited to high volumes.
Punching die; punch and dieقالب پانچ (سنبه و ماتریس)The custom steel tool that defines the hole shape, made once per design.
Stroke; hitضربOne cycle of the press; punching production is usually priced per stroke.
Bending; sheet metal bendingخم‌کاری (خمکاری)Changing the angle of a sheet along a straight line with a press and bending tools.
Press brakeپرس‌برک (پرس برک)A sheet bending press with an upper punch and a V die that makes straight, repeatable bends.
Coping; saddle cut; fish-mouth cutبرش دهان‌ماهی (زین‌اسبی)Shaping a tube end to the profile of the tube it meets, so the two sit tight and weld evenly.
Mitre cut (US: miter cut)برش فارسی‌بر (فارسی بر)An angled end cut for the corners of frames.
Tab and slot jointاتصال زبانه و شیارA tab on one part locates in a slot on another and holds the frame in position before welding.
Gusset; gusset plateلچکیA triangular or trapezoidal plate welded into a joint corner to stiffen it.
Rivet nut; blind rivet nut; rivnutمهره‌پرچ (مهره پرچی، ریوت‌نات)A nut riveted into a hole in thin sheet or a tube wall that gives a screw a strong thread.
Box section; square or rectangular hollow section (SHS / RHS)قوطیA hollow profile with a square or rectangular section.
Angle; L-sectionنبشیAn L-shaped profile with two legs.
Channel; C-channel or U-channelناودانیA U-shaped profile with a web and two flanges.

CNC machining and turning terms, including steel names

In machining, the process name, the thread and the steel grade are the words that must be right on the drawing. Iranian suppliers mostly use German trade names for steel, such as MO40 and CK45. HyperFab takes CNC turning as batch orders.

EnglishPersianShort explanation
CNC machiningماشین‌کاری CNCMaterial removal on a machine that follows a computer-generated toolpath.
CNC turning; lathe workتراشکاری CNC (تراش)Making round parts by rotating the workpiece against a cutting tool.
CNC millingفرزکاری (فرز CNC)Material removal with a rotating cutter for flat faces, pockets, slots and prismatic shapes.
Thread; threading; tapping (internal)رزوه (رزوه‌زنی، دنده)An internal or external helical groove for screws, nuts and pipe fittings.
Thread rolling; rolled threadرولینگ رزوه (نورد رزوه، رزوه نوردی)Forming an external thread by pressing the blank between threaded rolls, with no chips; the result is stronger than a cut thread.
Centerless grindingسنگ سنترلس (سنگ‌زنی بی‌مرغک)Precision grinding of a shaft’s outside diameter without centres, between the grinding wheel and a regulating wheel; used, for example, to bring thread-rolling blanks to an exact diameter.
Surface grindingسنگ تخت (سنگ‌زنی سطحی)Grinding a flat face with a wheel that passes back and forth over the part, for a flat, parallel and accurate surface; a common step before and after wire cutting die parts.
Wire EDM; wire cuttingوایرکات (اسپارک سیمی، برش سیمی)Cutting conductive metals with electrical sparks from a thin wire and no cutting force; it cuts hardened steel and leaves almost sharp inside corners.
Knurl; knurlingآج (آج‌زنی)A ridged pattern rolled or cut on a round surface for a better grip.
ChamferپخA small angled edge that removes sharpness and eases assembly.
Burr; deburringپلیسه (پلیسه‌گیری)The sharp edge left after cutting or machining, and the work of removing it.
Gauge (US: gage); go / no-go gaugeگیجA reference tool for quick checks of a size or thread, usually made from hardened steel.
42CrMo4 (1.7225), traded in Iran as MO40MO40A heat-treatable chromium-molybdenum alloy steel for loaded parts, critical shafts and gauges.
C45E (1.1191), traded in Iran as CK45CK45A cheaper medium-carbon steel, common for shafts and general parts.
Free-cutting brass; leaded brassبرنج خوش‌تراش (برنج سرب‌دار)Brass that breaks into short chips and machines fast and clean; the standard material for fittings.
Heat treatment; quench and temperعملیات حرارتی (کوئنچ و تمپر)Controlled heating and cooling of steel to reach the required hardness and toughness.

Mould making and injection moulding terms

Mould making (written قالب‌سازی or قالب سازی) and injection moulding come with a lot of vocabulary you will hear in design reviews and trial reports. HyperFab coordinates and carries out the whole chain, from mould design and toolmaking to moulded parts.

EnglishPersianShort explanation
Injection moulding (US: injection molding)تزریق پلاستیکInjecting molten plastic into a steel mould and ejecting the cooled part every cycle.
Injection mould (US: mold); mould making; toolmakingقالب تزریق (قالب‌سازی)The two-half steel tool that forms the part; mould making is designing and building it.
Cavityحفره (کویته)The space in the mould that forms the outside of the part; a multi-cavity mould makes several parts per cycle.
Coreکور (ماهیچه)The raised side of the mould that forms the inside of the part.
Parting lineخط جدایشWhere the two mould halves meet, usually leaving a faint line on the part.
Draft; draft angleزاویه‌ی خروج (شیب)A slight taper on walls parallel to the opening direction so the part releases cleanly.
Ribریب (دیواره‌ی تقویتی)A thin stiffening wall used instead of a thick section.
Gateنقطه‌ی تزریق (گیت)The opening where plastic enters the cavity, usually leaving a small mark.
Runner; cold runner / hot runnerراهگاه (راهگاه سرد و گرم)The channel that carries plastic from the machine nozzle to the gate.
Ejector pinپین بیرون‌انداز (پران)A pin that pushes the part out of the opened mould and leaves a small witness mark.
Undercutزیربرش (آندرکات)A feature that blocks straight ejection and needs a slide or side action.
Mould trial; T0 / T1 samplingنمونه‌گیری T0 و T1Trial shots from a new mould: the first to find problems, the next after corrections, for approval.
EDM (electrical discharge machining); sinker EDMاسپارک (اسپارک‌کاری)Shaping hardened mould steel with electrical sparks and an electrode, for sharp corners and fine detail.

Aluminium extrusion terms: dies and profiles

Iranian suppliers say both ekstrud and ekstruzhen; they mean the same process. HyperFab takes extrusion orders for aluminium only; the customer pays for the custom die and the order has a minimum tonnage.

EnglishPersianShort explanation
Extrusionاکستروژن (اکسترود)Forcing a hot aluminium billet through a die to make a profile with a constant section.
Aluminium profile (US: aluminum); extrusion sectionپروفیل آلومینیوم (مقطع)The extruded product, with the same cross-section along its whole length.
Extrusion dieقالب اکستروژنThe steel disc carrying the section shape, made specifically for each section.
Solid dieقالب توپرA die for sections with no enclosed space, such as angles, flats and channels.
Semi-hollow dieقالب نیمه‌توخالیA die for nearly enclosed sections with a narrow opening that puts the die tongue under load.
Hollow die; porthole or bridge dieقالب توخالیA die for sections with one or more enclosed voids, such as box section and tube.
Billetشمش (بیلت)The aluminium log that is heated and loaded into the press container.
Order tonnage; minimum order quantity (MOQ)تناژ سفارشTotal profile weight in an order; a custom extrusion only pays off above a minimum weight.
Press capacity; press tonnageظرفیت پرس (تناژ پرس)The force of the extrusion press, which limits the section size that can be run.
6000-series aluminium alloyآلیاژ سری ۶۰۰۰Aluminium-magnesium-silicon alloys, the most common for profiles and good for anodizing.
Temper (e.g. T5, T6)حالت عملیات حرارتی (تمپر)Part of the profile specification, written after the alloy, that sets its strength.

Welding and weld inspection terms: trade names vs standard names

Welding trade names often differ from the standard ones: "argon welding" means TIG, and "CO2 welding" is really MAG. HyperFab carries out TIG and MIG welding of stainless steel and aluminium to the customer's design and coordinates inspection as the project requires.

EnglishPersianShort explanation
TIG welding (GTAW)جوش آرگون (جوشکاری TIG)Welding with a non-consumable tungsten electrode and inert shielding gas for a clean, controlled bead.
MIG welding (GMAW with inert gas)جوش MIGWelding with continuously fed wire and inert gas; faster than TIG, used on aluminium and stainless.
MAG welding (GMAW with active gas)جوش CO2The same family as MIG with an active gas; common on carbon steel and not suitable for aluminium.
Filler metal; filler wire or rodسیم‌جوش (فیلر)Metal added to the weld pool, which must be compatible with the base metal.
Shielding gasگاز محافظGas that protects the weld pool from the air.
Heat tintتغییر رنگ حرارتیThe coloured oxide band beside a stainless weld that lowers corrosion resistance there.
PicklingاسیدشوییRemoving heat tint and surface oxides from stainless welds with an acid solution.
PassivationپسیواسیونRestoring the natural protective layer of stainless steel after welding, pickling or machining.
NDT (non-destructive testing)آزمون غیرمخربInspecting welds and parts without damaging them, by methods set in the drawing or contract.
VT (visual testing)بازرسی چشمیThe basis of every weld inspection: bead shape, undercut, cracks and surface porosity.
PT (penetrant testing; dye penetrant)مایع نافذRevealing surface-breaking cracks and discontinuities with a coloured liquid and developer.
PAUT (phased array ultrasonic testing)فراصوتی آرایه‌فازی (التراسونیک فیزد اَری)Finding internal defects such as lack of fusion and porosity with ultrasound.

Coating and finishing terms: anodizing, plating, chromate, powder coating

Finishes have the most spelling variants: anodizing appears as آندایز, آنودایز or آنادایز, and rang-e kure-i (oven paint) is the everyday name for electrostatic powder coating. HyperFab offers silver and gold plating on brass, anodizing, chromate conversion and powder coating for batch orders.

EnglishPersianShort explanation
Anodizing (UK: anodising)آندایز (آنودایز، آنادایز)Controlled growth of a hard, insulating oxide layer out of the aluminium itself in an electrolyte bath.
Chromate conversion coating; Alodine; chem film (MIL-DTL-5541)کروماته (آلوداین)A thin, conductive conversion coating on aluminium formed without electric current.
Electroplating; platingآبکاری (آبکاری الکتریکی)Depositing a layer of another metal on the part with electric current in a plating bath.
Silver platingآبکاری نقرهThe most conductive plating, for contacts and electrical parts; it tarnishes over time.
Gold platingآبکاری طلاA non-oxidising finish with stable contact resistance, usually over an underlayer.
Powder coating; electrostatic powder coatingرنگ کوره‌ای (رنگ پودری الکترواستاتیک، رنگ پودری)Spraying charged dry powder onto the part and curing it in an oven into a continuous, tough film.
Pearl / metallic powderرنگ صدفی و متالیکPowders with fine reflective particles whose look shifts with viewing angle and light.
RAL colour codeکد رنگ RALThe common colour numbering system used to specify powder coat colours on drawings and orders.
Gloss, semi-gloss, matt, textured finishبراق، نیم‌براق، مات، بافت‌دارThe gloss level and texture, which must be stated separately from the colour code.
Maskingماسک‌کاریCovering threads, contact faces or earthing points that must stay uncoated.
Surface preparation; pretreatment; degreasing; sandblastingآماده‌سازی سطح (چربی‌گیری، سندبلاست)Cleaning and preparing the surface before coating, which decides adhesion and durability.
Adhesion test; cross-cut test (ISO 2409)آزمون چسبندگی (آزمون شطرنجی)Scoring a lattice into the coating and checking for flaking to judge adhesion.

3D printing terms

3D printing (written پرینت سه‌بعدی or پرینت سه بعدی) is one of HyperFab's online services; you upload the part file at hyperfab.ir. These are the words that come up most when choosing a process and setting up a print.

EnglishPersianShort explanation
3D printing; additive manufacturingپرینت سه‌بعدی (چاپ سه‌بعدی)Building a part layer by layer straight from a 3D model, with no tooling.
FDM (fused deposition modeling); FFFFDM (پرینت فیلامنتی)Melting and laying down plastic filament; suited to prototypes, fixtures and functional parts.
SLA / DLP; resin printingSLA و DLP (پرینت رزینی)Curing liquid resin with UV light; suited to small parts with fine detail and a smooth surface.
FilamentفیلامنتThe plastic strand, such as PLA, PETG, ABS or TPU, that feeds an FDM printer.
Layer heightضخامت لایهThe thickness of each printed layer; thinner layers give a smoother surface and a longer print.
Infill; infill densityمیزان پرشدگی (اینفیل)How solid the inside of the part is, which affects weight, strength and print time.
Walls; perimeters; shellsدیواره (پوسته)The solid outer layers of the part, which carry much of its strength.
Support; support structureساپورتTemporary structure under overhangs, removed after printing and leaving light marks.
Build orientation; print orientationجهت چاپHow the part sits on the build plate, which affects strength, surface quality and support use.
FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Why do Iranian shops use different names for the same process?
Many shop terms come from different languages, from machine brand names or from what the work looks like: dahan-mahi (fish-mouth) and zin-asbi (saddle) both describe a coped tube end, and "argon welding" is named after the gas. Spellings of borrowed words such as anodizing have never been standardised in Persian. To avoid misunderstandings, put the standard or English name next to the trade name on drawings and quote requests.
Which file formats should I send?
For 3D parts such as turned parts, moulds and tube or profile laser cutting, STEP or IGES is the best input, and an STL is enough for 3D printing. For sheet laser cutting and punching, a 2D DXF is the norm. Either way, add a PDF drawing with tolerances, material, finish and quantity.
Is every glossary term a service HyperFab offers?
Not necessarily; the glossary is meant for reading drawings and talking to any supplier, and some terms are here only for reference. The full list is on HyperFab's services page.
